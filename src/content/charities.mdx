### Why giving matters to me

I've put this page together to remind myself of the nonprofit causes that I never want to forget, which have supported me in one way or another in the past. It is less about numbers and more about stories, gratitude, and the ways I want to keep showing up.

I hope to inspire you (yes, you, reading this) to also think back to a time where a charity/nonprofit has directly impacted you and give back, so they can continue to create a positive impact in our communities.

### A few things I care about

- People and communities that shaped my life
- Education and opportunities for people who start with less
  - Tech education for underrepresented students
- Local efforts where I can actually see the impact
- Moments when I was helped, and chances to pay that forward
